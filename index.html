About Beta

From a small primary school to a Higher Secondary institution.

The school was started on 13th June 1990 by the KREPSMAJC Education Trust as a primary school and has since grown into a Higher Secondary institution. It is co-educational, recognised by the Government of Tamil Nadu, and ISO 9001 certified. The trust is run by Beta's executive council and advisory committee.

We work for the moral, intellectual and cultural development of every child. English is the medium of instruction for all classes, and Tamil and Hindi are taught as 2nd and 3rd languages for Classes I to V.

General rules

Discipline & conduct
  • Every pupil is expected to live up to the School Motto, maintaining good behaviour and discipline.
  • All students should converse in English on the school premises.
  • Habitual idleness, neglect of work, cheating, disobedience, disrespect to staff, copying in exams, or bad moral behaviour are sufficient reasons for dismissal.
  • Pupils must keep classrooms and school premises clean and tidy.
  • Students causing damage to school property must compensate the institution, at the discretion of the Correspondent / Principal.
  • Students must read and follow the instructions on the notice board.
Attendance & punctuality
  • Students must be inside the school at least 15 minutes before the first bell. Late comers will be marked absent.
  • Attendance is taken five minutes after each session begins.
  • Attend classes punctually with lessons prepared, and bring books and notebooks as per the timetable.
  • Half-day leave or one-hour permission is not allowed on school or exam days.
  • Leave of more than 3 consecutive days due to illness requires a medical certificate.
  • No leave without prior permission on the reopening day after I / II Summative exams. Otherwise a ₹25 penalty applies and entry needs the Correspondent's permission.
Uniform, diary & belongings
  • Come to school neat and tidy in the prescribed uniform.
  • Bring the school diary every day. Announcements and homework are recorded in it.
  • Pupils are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • Expensive jewellery or ornaments are not allowed and are worn at the parent's own risk.
  • Textbooks and notebooks should be purchased only from the school.
  • Students of Std VI and above may come to school by bicycle.
Lunch & health
  • Bring lunch daily with napkins / hand towels, table mats and spoons. Table manners must be followed.
  • If a child forgets lunch, parents should write the child's name, class and section on a paper and hand it with the lunch bag to the Ayyah before 11 a.m.
  • Pupils with contagious or infectious diseases may not attend school.
  • Parents should not ask about a child's fitness or leave over the phone. If a child is unwell, parents should come in person and give the medicine.
For parents & visitors
  • Inform the school whenever your address changes.
  • Check the notice board for holidays declared by the Government due to rain or other reasons.
  • Do not park vehicles in front of the main entrance.
  • Keep fee receipts safely and show them when asked. A lost bank challan or report card can be replaced for ₹25.
  • Parents, visitors and outsiders are allowed on campus only in proper dress code.
  • Servants may enter the premises only between 12.35–12.45 p.m. and 3.30–3.45 p.m.
  • Bonafide certificates and photo attestation are done by the Principal only for photos taken in school uniform. Collect them 2 days after submission.
